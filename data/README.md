# Synthetic data

Everything here is generated by `scripts/generate_data.py` with a fixed seed, so every run
produces the same files. All people, policies, carriers, body shops, clinics, police
departments, addresses and VINs are invented. Damage "photos" are simple drawn
illustrations, labeled as synthetic in the image itself. Every PDF carries a footer that
says it is synthetic.

| File | Rows | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `csv/policies.csv` | 105 | Personal auto policies: coverages, deductibles, limits, inception dates |
| `csv/claims.csv` | 105 | Claims reported between Oct 20 and Nov 18, 2026 |
| `csv/documents.csv` | 306 | Intake manifest: which claim each file belongs to, when and how it arrived |
| `csv/claim_notes.csv` | 92 | Adjuster and intake notes |
| `csv/adjusters.csv` | 8 | Adjusters, skills, capacity and current open claims |
| `csv/loss_history.csv` | 3 | Loss-history report rows for prior losses with other carriers |
| `csv/closed_claims.csv` | 672 | Closed claims over 12 months, for the cycle-time trend |
| `documents/` | 306 | 105 FNOL emails, 51 claim forms, 98 repair estimates, 31 police report excerpts, 14 medical bill summaries, 7 damage illustrations |

`documents.csv` has a `doc_type` label from the generator. The pipeline never reads it; setup
only uses it to measure how often AI_CLASSIFY agrees.

## Planted stories

| Claim | Story | What the pipeline surfaces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CLM-24003 | Theft reported 2 days after the policy was bound; the loss-history report shows 3 prior losses (2 thefts) with three carriers, and the police report was filed 3 days late | SIU referral tier, red flags in the AI summary |
| CLM-24004 | Windshield crack from road debris, full-glass coverage, $412 estimate, photo attached | Fast track tier, $0 deductible in the coverage check |
| CLM-24005 | Rear-end collision; the passenger's medical bills climb from $1,240 to $3,860, $7,950 and $14,300 | Complex tier, bills in date order exceed the $5,000 med pay limit |
| CLM-24008 | Repair estimate whose stated total ($5,229) is $1,250 more than its line items ($3,979) | "Estimate total ≠ line items" flag; the AI summary asks for a corrected estimate (AI_CLASSIFY put it in Standard on our run) |

Regenerate with `python3 scripts/generate_data.py` (needs `reportlab` and `Pillow`).
